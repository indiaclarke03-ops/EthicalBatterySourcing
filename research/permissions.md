**Self-imposed limits that still apply, even with full scope** (CONCEPT-IDEA_1.md commitments 3 and 4):
- Supplier-level forced-labour flags are published in aggregate unless a credible public investigation corroborates them.
- Flags are labelled as screening results, not allegations.
- Findings matching known Sayari artefact patterns are suppressed and noted on the methods page.
- Raw bulk exports aren't committed. Notes cite Sayari entity URLs (`graph.sayari.com/resource/entity/<id>`) so each finding can be checked.

**Citation format:** `Sayari Graph, <entity or record name>, entity <id>, retrieved <YYYY-MM-DD>`.
